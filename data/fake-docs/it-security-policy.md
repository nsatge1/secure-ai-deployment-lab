# IT Security Policy

> Fictional document created for the Secure AI Deployment Lab. Bramblecove Analytics is an imaginary company. No real data.

## Scope

This policy applies to every employee, contractor and intern of Bramblecove Analytics, on every device used for work, including personal phones that access company email.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase made of several unrelated words is recommended. Passwords must never be shared, including with colleagues, managers or the IT team: the IT team will never ask for your password.

The same password must not be reused across services. Employees must use the company password manager to store their credentials.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, the HR portal and every cloud service. Hardware security keys are provided to administrators. SMS codes are not accepted as a second factor for administrator accounts.

## Devices

Laptops must be encrypted, locked when unattended and updated within 7 days of a security update being published. Installing software from unofficial sources is forbidden. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 1 hour.

## Remote access

Company systems are only reachable through the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN enabled.

## Phishing

Suspicious emails must be reported with the "Report phishing" button, or forwarded to security@bramblecove.example. Never click a link or open an attachment you did not expect, even if the sender seems to be a colleague.
