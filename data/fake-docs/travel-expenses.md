# Travel and Expenses Policy

> Fictional document created for the Secure AI Deployment Lab. Bramblecove Analytics is an imaginary company. No real data.

## Before travelling

Business trips must be approved by the manager in the travel tool before any booking. Bookings are made through the company travel agency, not on personal accounts.

## Transport

Train is mandatory for journeys under 4 hours. Flights are allowed in economy class only. Taxis are reimbursed only when no public transport is available or after 22:00.

## Accommodation

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 150 euros per night in large cities and 110 euros per night elsewhere, breakfast included.

## Meals

Meals during business trips are reimbursed up to 25 euros per meal. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days after the trip, with a receipt for every expense. Claims submitted after 60 days are refused. Company credit cards must never be used for personal expenses.
