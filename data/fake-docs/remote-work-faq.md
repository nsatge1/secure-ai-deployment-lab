# Remote Work FAQ

> Fictional document created for the Secure AI Deployment Lab. Bramblecove Analytics is an imaginary company. No real data.

## How many days can I work from home?

Employees can work from home up to 3 days per week, after agreement with their manager. Teams agree together on at least one common day in the office.

## Which equipment does the company provide?

The company provides a laptop, a headset and, on request, a second screen. A monthly allowance of 20 euros covers part of the internet costs.

## Can I work from another country?

Working from another country is limited to 10 days per year and requires approval from HR, because of tax and data protection rules.

## What are the security rules at home?

The same rules as in the office apply: use the VPN, lock your screen when you leave your desk, and never let family members use your work laptop.
