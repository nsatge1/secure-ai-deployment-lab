# Security Incident Response Procedure

> Fictional document created for the Secure AI Deployment Lab. Bramblecove Analytics is an imaginary company. No real data.

## What is a security incident?

A security incident is any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity or availability of company data or systems. Examples: a malware infection, a lost laptop, a password typed into a phishing page, or data sent to the wrong recipient.

## Severity levels

- **Low**: no data exposed, limited impact. Example: a phishing email reported but not clicked.
- **Medium**: one account or device compromised, no confirmed data leak.
- **High**: confirmed data leak, several systems compromised, or a critical service unavailable.

## First steps for any employee

1. Do not turn off the affected device: disconnect it from the network instead.
2. Report the incident immediately to the security team at security@bramblecove.example or on the internal incident line, extension 4242.
3. Write down what happened and when, while you still remember the details.
4. Do not try to investigate or clean the device yourself, and do not delete any file.

## What the security team does

The security team confirms the incident, assigns a severity level and contains it (isolating devices, resetting passwords, blocking accounts). For high-severity incidents, the Chief Information Security Officer is informed within 1 hour and the legal team decides whether authorities or customers must be notified.

## After the incident

Every medium or high incident ends with a review meeting within 10 working days. The review identifies the root cause and the actions that prevent it from happening again. The goal is to learn, not to blame.
