# Onboarding Guide for New Employees

> Fictional document created for the Secure AI Deployment Lab. Bramblecove Analytics is an imaginary company. No real data.

## Before your first day

You receive a welcome email with your start time and the address of your office. Your laptop is prepared by the IT team and handed to you on your first morning.

## Your first day

- Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy, a colleague who answers your questions during your first month.
- Collect your laptop and your hardware security key if your role requires one.
- Activate your accounts and set up multi-factor authentication with the IT team.

## Your first week

All new employees must complete the mandatory training within their first 5 working days:

- Security awareness (phishing, passwords, reporting incidents).
- Data protection and confidentiality.
- Code of conduct.

Access to production systems is granted only after the security awareness training is completed.

## Your first month

At the end of your first month, you have a review meeting with your manager to discuss your objectives and any support you need.
