# Leave Policy

> Fictional document created for the Secure AI Deployment Lab. Bramblecove Analytics is an imaginary company. No real data.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a number of days proportional to their working time. New employees receive leave in proportion to the months remaining in the year.

## Requesting leave

Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for periods of 3 days or more. Requests for 1 or 2 days need 2 working days of notice. The manager approves or refuses the request within 5 working days.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be taken before March 31, otherwise they are lost.

## Sick leave

Employees who are sick must inform their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of absence.

## Other leave

- Marriage or civil partnership: 4 days.
- Birth or adoption of a child: as defined by law, plus 2 additional days offered by the company.
- Death of a close family member: 3 days.
- Moving house: 1 day per year.
